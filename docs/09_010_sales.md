# 010 Hardware Sales (ChannelOnline + SL)

The 010 division (IT hardware "box sales" — GL subs 0100/0101, ≈$19–25M revenue/yr, thin margins)
is quoted and ordered in **ChannelOnline (CNET)** and fulfilled/invoiced in **Dynamics SL** as SO1
sales orders. PCA joins the two: CNET holds the quote-time story (who quoted what, at what cost and
price, to whom, when), SL holds the fulfillment truth (what actually shipped, invoiced, and cost).
Requirements of record: Owner's 8 answers in `docs/08_channelonline_exploration.md` §Q&A; plan in
`../Pace_Company_Analytics_010_Sales_Spec_v1.md`.

## Data flow

```
CNET XML API ──(cnet_client → cnet_parse → loaders.upsert_documents)──▶ sales_cnetdocument(+line,+serial)
SL SOHeader/SOShipHeader/SOLine ──(sl_loaders.load_sl_cnet, full replace)──▶ sales_slcnetorder/shipper/orderline
SL AcctHist 0100+0101 ──(sl_loaders.load_gl_010)──▶ sales_gl010period
                └──▶ sl_loaders.build_link_and_econ: SL link, realized econ, per-line cost match,
                     quote→order conversion, hygiene counts   (refresh_all step "sales_link_econ")
```

- **Join key**: `SOHeader.User2` = CNET documentNumber (`sales_slcnetorder.cnet_number`, char(30) with
  occasional junk). CNET `customerNumber` = SL `CustId` → `core_customer`.
- **Upsert key**: CNET `documentId` (stable across revisions; highest revision wins; content-hash
  short-circuits unchanged docs). Lines are replaced on change; SL-link/realized fields survive re-import.
- **Nightly**: `refresh_all` runs `sl_cnet`, `gl_010_pnl`, then `cnet_incremental` (last-45-days
  created+modified windows, both doc types; **non-fatal** — a CNET outage never sinks the refresh;
  a window that overflows the API's 500-document cap — error 507, routine since 2026-08-28 — is bisected
  automatically by `pull_range`, `splits` in the step stats),
  and `sales_link_econ` in the analytics section.
- **Backfill**: `manage.py cnet_backfill` (month chunks 2013-01→now, resumable via the gzip archive in
  `APP_SUPPORT_DIR/cnet_raw/`, ~2.5 s pacing + retries; takes hours). Read-only against CNET; never
  emits the PTT importer's lock/mark flags.

## Definitions that matter (why they are what they are)

- **Bookings** = CNET SO1 sales orders by `ordered_at` (CNET quote-time totals).
- **Invoiced / realized GP** = Σ SL shippers (`tot_invc`, `tot_invc − tot_cost`) by invoice date — SL is
  fulfillment truth.
- **Both margins + drift**: quoted GM% (CNET price vs CNET cost) vs real GM% (SL invoiced vs shipped
  cost); **drift = SL shipped cost − CNET quoted cost**, order-level and per-line (part match by
  `UPPER(SOLine.InvtID) = UPPER(part_number)`), with rep × creator attribution.
- **Backlog = SL `SOHeader.Status='O'`**, not CNET status. CNET order statuses are frequently never
  updated (2013 orders still say "Submitted"); SL had exactly 265 SO1s open when built, all 2025+.
- **Open pipeline is age-bounded to 180 days** (`views_sales.PIPE_DAYS`): CNET never expires abandoned
  quotes (2013 quotes still carry status "Open"), so older unconverted quotes are treated as abandoned
  — counted separately on the pipeline page, never in the KPI.
- **Quote→order conversion has no structural link in CNET.** Matched by (1) "QUOTE #123456" references
  in notes (`note_ref`), (2) same-customer total-±2 %/60-day heuristic (`heuristic_total`); the method
  is stored and shown. Full-history vocabulary (2026-08-29): 99.8 % of quotes stay "Open" forever
  (35,733 Open / 55 Lost / 10 Expired / 3 Hold) — `TERMINAL_QUOTE` is a harmless superset; conversion
  status + the age window do the real work.
- **GL tie-out**: `sales_gl010period` (AcctHist natural signs — revenue credit-positive, NO flip;
  4\*=revenue, 50750/50760=freight, 5\*=cogs). The overview footnote shows GL revenue/COGS/GP next to
  shipper GP; when they diverge (2026 FYTD: GL GP ≈$225k vs shipper GP ≈$1.2M) the GL wins — the gap is
  freight/variance/inventory entries and non-CNET postings, and is worth asking about.

### Quote pipeline (`/sales/010/pipeline/`, `apps/dashboard/views_sales_pipeline.py`, rebuilt 2026-09-01)

One filtered window drives the whole page: created-date range (presets 30/90/180/365/YTD/all or explicit
from/to; default `PIPE_DAYS`) × outcome × rep × author (`created_by_name`) × sector × customer × size band ×
free text × manufacturer (EXISTS on lines) × customer-built (author e-mail outside pace-systems.com). Every
breakdown table links back into the filters (click a rep / customer / sector / manufacturer / size band /
author to focus the page). The page is one CTE (`w`) queried ~12 ways; ≈150 ms for 180 days, ≈1 s for "all".

- **Outcome** per quote: won = `converted_document_id` set (or status Ordered); lost = status Lost / Expired /
  Dead / Closed / Cancelled; hold = Hold; else open. Win rate = won ÷ created in the window (count and $),
  never won ÷ decided (reps never mark quotes lost).
- **Jumbo gate**: quotes ≥ `JUMBO_AMT` ($1M) are out of every total by default (the "include ≥ $1M" checkbox
  puts them back) and listed in their own card — one $230M Bechtel placeholder with zero lines otherwise
  swamps the division. Size bands table always shows the ≥ $1M band so the excluded set is visible.
- **Cost sanity** (`COST_SANITY` = 1.5× price): CNET costs are hand-typed; a few quotes carry a unit-vs-
  extended error (a $165M cost on a $550k quote). Such quotes stay in $ totals but out of every GM figure
  and show a `cost?` tag; quotes with no cost show "—". The open-pipeline tile says how many quotes the GM
  rests on.
- **Stale** = open with no CNET created/modified activity for `STALE_DAYS` (30). Row tint: open > 30 d amber,
  > 60 d red. Quotes with a total but zero lines ("no lines" tag, `?nolines=1`) are lump sums.
- **Quote table** is client-rendered from an embedded JSON blob (cap `ROW_CAP` = 1,500 rows, honest note when
  capped): column sort, in-page text filter, and **click-to-expand** rows that fetch
  `/sales/010/pipeline/quote/<number>/` (`sales010_quote_json`, cap `sales010.view`) for header, notes, totals,
  by-manufacturer rollup, every line with quoted cost/price/GP/GM, and — when won — the linked order's SL /
  realized summary with the link method. Panels are cached per quote; the expanded panel is width-locked
  (`.qx-wrap`) so a 12-column line table never widens the quote table.
- Charts (single axis each, palette validated): quotes created per week/month stacked by outcome
  (won #178a52 / open #1f5eff / lost+hold #b7791f) and open $ by rep × age bucket (sequential blues).
- Caveat carried on the page: a `heuristic_total` match can link several near-identical quotes to one order,
  so won counts can double up; `note_ref` is exact.

## Gotchas

- CNET userAgent must be exactly `PACEXMLTEST`; auth from `.env` (never printed); errors arrive as
  `<error>` inside HTTP 200; payloads contain control chars (parser uses recover=True); rapid requests
  drop connections (client paces ≥2.5 s + retries).
- `sales_slcnetorder.cnet_number` is SL User2 char(30) junk-tolerant; truncated to 30.
- Never join CNET docs to SL by customer alone; document number is the only reliable key.
- Mid-backfill, hygiene "In SL but not in CNET" is inflated by not-yet-loaded history; it shrinks to
  true typos when the backfill completes.

### Customer page card (`/customers/<id>/` → "010 hardware quotes & orders", 2026-09-08)
Owner: "I know there are some large SO1 quotes, but I don't see them on this page." The customer page showed SL projects
only, so a customer like X-Energy (96 open quotes, $11.5M) looked idle. `views_sales_pipeline.customer_sales010` now
feeds a card for `sales010.view` holders (computed only for them): every ChannelOnline quote for the SL customer, all
time, through the pipeline's own CTE (`_base`) so outcome / age / stale / jumbo mean exactly what they mean on the
pipeline page — with one deliberate difference: quotes ≥ $1M are counted in full here (their own chip says so), because
this is the customer's picture rather than the company funnel. Chips: open pipeline (≤ `PIPE_DAYS`, stale count),
aged out (older, still "Open" in CNET), won (with all-time win rate by count and $, and the last 12 months), orders
(cancelled excluded; backlog = SL status O, not cancelled, total − invoiced). Tables: all quotes (chip filter open /
aged / won / lost-hold remembered per browser, text filter, ten-row own scroll; the quote number opens the pipeline
filtered to it) and all sales orders (SL status, from-quote, invoiced, realized GM, link to the order page). Cost and
GM show to every `sales010.view` holder, as on the 010 pages.
