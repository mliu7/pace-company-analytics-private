Corporate overhead allocation

How the corporate (000) overhead pool is charged to divisions in the P&L-by-fiscal-year table. The pool = GL subaccount 0000 overhead (6xxxx/7xxxx, excluding 60000/60005 which are cost of sales). Shares are set here per fiscal year (the FY2024 and FY2025 splits were entered by hand). A year without explicit shares inherits the latest earlier year, so the current year follows FY2025's split until you change it. Shares don't have to sum to 100% — the remainder stays at corporate. Stored locally only; SL is never written.
