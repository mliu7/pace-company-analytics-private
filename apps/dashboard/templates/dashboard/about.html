Definitions & how to read the numbers

Every figure traces to SL account categories and PTT entries; nothing is written back to either system.

Economics (Dynamics SL)

Contract value
SL CONTRACT VALUE budget, summed over tasks. SL keeps only the current value; "budget edited" shows the last edit date of that row.
Billed revenue
SL REVENUE actuals = AR invoices − credits. SL does not post percent-complete revenue to projects.
Earned revenue
Contract value × PM % complete (PTT). Derived, labelled.
Labor
Wages (LABOR + LABORUNION) + burden (BURDEN: employer payroll tax per employee + union fringe vouchers per project).
Material / Subcontract / Other direct
MATERIALS · SUBCONTRACT · ODC+TRAVEL (freight is inside ODC, GL 50750/50760).
Purchase variance
PURCHASEVARIANCE: vendor invoice vs PO/receipt price on the job's purchases — a correction of material cost (usually negative). Shown as its own component so the direct-cost breakdown adds up; folded into material in the EAC and in the cost mix.
Open commitments
Open PO lines (ordered − received × unit cost) + warehouse stock received for the job and not yet shipped to it. Not SL's raw com_amount: at Pace that figure is mostly project-inventory allocations created by PO receipts that are never relieved when the stock ships to the job through Order Management, so it double-counts cost already in actuals (the "already shipped" amount is shown separately on each project).
Direct cost / GP
Sum of the above; GP = billed revenue − direct cost; GP % = GP ÷ billed revenue. Matches SL's own PJPrjBgt.ACT_Margin.
Sold GP
Contract value − current budget direct cost (labor budget is loaded; burden rarely budgeted separately). Current-budget basis — original budgets do not exist in SL.
Margin preservation
Final GP % − sold GP %, in points, revenue-weighted across jobs with a contract value.
Loaded labor rate
Labor (wages + burden) ÷ SL labor-charge hours (PA/PR/TM rows only — PO quantities on labor accounts are excluded).

Operations (PTT)

PTT hours
Live Job Report entries: on-site + OT + off-site. Time-off entries never count. Ties to SL labor hours at 99.98 % in aggregate.
PM remaining hours
PTT estimated_hours_to_completion (union + non-union), with every revision since Aug 2019.
PM % complete
PTT's labor-weighted blend of hours-remaining and expense-remaining (written back to SL PM_ID26 by PTT).
Labor % (calc)
PTT hours ÷ (PTT hours + PM remaining hours).
System / Work type
Structured choices on every PTT entry; the project's solution class is the hour-weighted dominant system (title keywords as fallback).
Crew lead
Inferred: submitted PTT time for two or more other people on the project.

Lifecycle

Not Started
Awarded but not started: SL active, no PTT or SL hours, has contract value or budget hours.
In Progress
SL active with hours in the last 30 days, or remaining hours and work in the last 45 days.
Field Complete
SL active, no work in 30 days, remaining hours 0 or PM % ≥ 98.
Dormant
SL active, remaining hours > 0, no work in 45+ days.
Closed / Stabilizing → Closed
SL inactive; stabilized after 45 days without a material posting. Close date = PTT inactivation date, else SL last-edit at the flip.

Forecast & risk

EAC labor
Posted labor + unposted PTT hours × rate + PM remaining hours × rate. Rate hierarchy: project actual (≥80 h) → crew mix (13-week employee wage + tax + fringe) → division rolling → budget rate → $90 default.
EAC non-labor
Material: max(actual + purchase variance + real open commitments, budget); subcontract: max(actual + open commitments, budget); other: max(actual, budget).
EAC revenue
Contract value (T&M/service: max(billed, CV)).
Risk score
Heuristic 0–100: projected loss (+45), margin <10 % (+25), >5 pts below sold (+25), >15 pts (+10), hours EAC >110 % of budget (+15), >130 % (+10), dormant (+10), no PM estimate (+5). Low <25, moderate <50, high <75, critical ≥75 or projected loss > $50k.
