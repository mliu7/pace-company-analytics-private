How the model works

PeopleAccounts, pre-created in People & Access. Each person is assigned one or more roles. Nothing else is ever attached to a person — except a superadmin's extra grants.
RolesNamed bundles of capabilities: Executive, Division Manager, Finance, Project Manager, Estimator, Sales, Permission Admin. What each holds is decision 2.
AreasProjects, Finance, Bids… Every page in an area requires that area's capabilities; the few exceptions are spelled out. What each area requires is decision 3.
ConcealmentSuperadmin-tier capabilities are yours alone: their pages answer 404 to everyone else and their names never render. Nothing to decide — it is the rule you confirmed under sign-in.
The superadmin flag (you) holds every capability. Roles can never carry a superadmin-tier capability; a person can be given one with an extra grant on their console page.
